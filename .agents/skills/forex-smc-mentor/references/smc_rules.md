# Smart Money Concepts (SMC) & ICT Entry Models

This reference outlines the institutional trading models used by the `forex-smc-mentor` skill.

---

## 1. ICT Model 2022 (Silver Bullet / Displacement Model)

### Entry Conditions:
1. **Liquidity Sweep**: Price sweeps a prominent high (BSL) or low (SSL) during a Killzone session.
2. **Displacement**: Fast, high-volume impulse move away from the swept level, leaving a Fair Value Gap (FVG).
3. **Change of Character (CHoCH)**: Displacement closes beyond the recent swing low (for bearish) or swing high (for bullish).
4. **Retracement**: Price retraces back into the FVG (or 62-79% OTE level).
5. **Entry**: Limit order at the FVG boundary; Stop Loss placed above/below the displacement origin.
6. **Target**: Opposing liquidity pool (Minimum 1:2 Risk-to-Reward).

## 2. Judas Swing (London / NY False Break)

### Concept:
During the first 30–60 minutes of the London Open (07:00–08:00 UTC / 13:00–14:00 BST) or NY Open (12:30–13:30 UTC / 18:30–19:30 BST), institutional market makers manipulate price against the true daily bias to trigger stop orders and accumulate positions before the real expansion begins.

### Identification:
- If Daily Bias is **BULLISH**: Expect a morning drop to sweep the Asian Session Low into a Discount Order Block $\rightarrow$ Look for a long entry once rejection wicks form.
- If Daily Bias is **BEARISH**: Expect a morning rally to sweep the Asian Session High into a Premium Order Block $\rightarrow$ Look for a short entry.

---

## 3. Order Block (OB) Validation Checklist

Not every opposite-color candle is an Order Block. A high-probability OB must satisfy:
1. **Displacement**: Creates an energetic move with an unmitigated FVG immediately following it.
2. **Structure Break**: Causes a Break of Structure (BOS) on the 15m or higher timeframe.
3. **Freshness**: Has not been tapped yet (0 touches).
4. **Context**: Resides in Discount for Longs, or Premium for Shorts.

---

## 4. Fair Value Gap (FVG) Anatomy

- **Bullish FVG (BISI - Buy-Side Imbalance, Sell-Side Inefficiency)**:
  - Candle 1 High < Candle 3 Low.
  - The gap between Candle 1 High and Candle 3 Low is the imbalance zone where smart money will inject liquidity on a retest.
- **Bearish FVG (SIBI - Sell-Side Imbalance, Buy-Side Inefficiency)**:
  - Candle 1 Low > Candle 3 High.
  - The gap between Candle 1 Low and Candle 3 High serves as resistance.
- **Consequent Encroachment (CE)**: 50% midpoint of the FVG. Often acts as the exact institutional turnaround price.
